**Visual:** ChatSprint logo, then the full desktop control room.

**Narration:** “Twitch communities love playing together, but streamers often need several disconnected tools. ChatSprint turns ordinary Twitch chat into a multilingual live quiz with automatic scoring and a broadcast-ready overlay.”

**Narration:** “The correct option, each viewer’s verdict, scores, streak bonuses, and the leaderboard appear together. The OBS browser source stays synchronized without exposing control buttons.”
